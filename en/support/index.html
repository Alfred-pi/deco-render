Photo and render

How do I take a good photo?

Let in as much light as you can and hold the iPhone straight.

  • Interior: stand in a corner of the room and include the floor.
  • Facade: step back until you can see all of it.
  • Garden: take the photo from the house.

You can also import a photo you already took from Photos.

How do I choose what to redo and what to keep?

Once the photo is taken, the app splits it into zones: sofa, floor, walls, roof, lawn. Tap an element to redo it. Hold your finger on an element to keep it as it is. The sentence at the bottom of the screen writes itself as you go, then ends with the style or colour you pick.

Windows, doors and buildings always stay in place. If no zone is recognised in the photo, you choose the elements from a list.

The render is not what I expected. What can I do?

Renders are generated by AI from your photo: they keep the structure of the place but may interpret some details. A few things to try:

  • retake the photo with more light and a wider frame;
  • keep the elements that matter to you by holding them before starting the render;
  • ask for a variant, or retouch a single element from the result.
How many renders can I make?

On the app’s sample photo, renders are prepared in advance and marked “Exemple”: they show instantly and are never counted. On your own photos, each render is computed for you: the first 3 are free, then Deco Render Pro gives you up to 50 per week (weekly plan) or 60 per month (yearly plan).

When is the video tour coming?

The video tour is marked “Bientôt” (coming soon) in the app: it is in preparation and not available yet. No date has been set.

Not to be confused with the before/after video, which is already available: from a result, tap Vidéo to create a vertical clip to share.

Which languages does the app support?

The app interface is in French for now.

3D scan

Which iPhones support 3D scanning?

3D scanning uses the LiDAR sensor, found on iPhone 12 Pro, iPhone 12 Pro Max and the Pro and Pro Max models released since. It is for interiors only.

On other iPhones, the whole photo journey works, and a sample room that has already been measured lets you explore the plan and the budget. Deco Render requires iOS 17 or later.

How do I get a good scan?

Turn the lights on, tap “Commencer le scan”, aim at a wall, then turn slowly on the spot. Pass over the doors, windows and furniture. Before finishing, face the room again: this view is used for the render. Allow about a minute.

How reliable are the measurements and the budget?

The measurements give a good order of magnitude for preparing a project, but they do not replace a survey by a professional. The budget is an indicative range, computed from the quantities and from sample prices: it is not a quote. Have the dimensions checked and the works priced by a tradesperson before ordering.

Subscription and purchases

Why is there a watermark on my images?

On the free plan, the renders and videos you export carry a watermark. Deco Render Pro removes it. That is the only difference between the two plans.

An image already saved to Photos keeps its watermark: after moving to Pro, save it again from the project.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Deco Render. There you can change plan or cancel the subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. You keep Deco Render Pro until the end of the period already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

In the app, open the Account tab (“Compte”) and tap “Restaurer les achats”. Use the Apple account the subscription was purchased with.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, which decides on refunds. Make your request at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Projects and data

Where are my projects stored?

On your iPhone, in the app. There is no account and no sync, and projects are not included in iCloud backups: they do not move to a new iPhone and they disappear if you delete the app.

To keep a render, save it to Photos or share it. To keep a plan and its budget, send yourself the PDF report.

Are my photos sent anywhere?

Reading the photo and 3D scanning happen on the iPhone. When you ask for a render, the photo you chose and the style brief are sent to the image generation provider, only to produce the image. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

To delete a project, open it from the Projects tab (“Projets”) and tap the delete button: its photos and renders are erased. Deleting the app erases all projects.

Images saved to Photos are deleted from Photos. The publisher keeps neither your photos nor your projects on a server.

Is the app available on iPad or Android?

No. Deco Render is designed for iPhone, with iOS 17 or later.
