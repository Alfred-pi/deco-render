In short. Deco Render works without an account, without ads, without trackers and without analytics. Reading your photos and 3D scanning both happen on your iPhone. A single upload leaves the device: when you ask for a render, the image to transform and the style brief are sent to the image generation provider, only to produce the image. Your projects stay on your iPhone.

Test version

This policy describes the test version of Deco Render, distributed through TestFlight, as it stands on 23 November 2026. The “Test version” boxes apply to that version only. The policy will be updated before the app is released on the App Store.

Who is responsible

The data controller is the publisher of Deco Render, an independent developer based in Switzerland. This policy applies to the Deco Render app for iPhone and to this website.

The contact address is being set up. It will be shown here.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Reading the photo. Splitting the photo into zones (sofa, floor, walls, roof, lawn) is done by a model built into the app, and recognising the type of room is done by the image analysis tools in iOS. Both steps are computed on the iPhone: nothing is sent.
  • The 3D scan. The measurements taken with the LiDAR sensor, the dimensioned plan, the 3D model, the quantities, the indicative budget and the PDF report are computed on the iPhone.
  • Your projects. The photos, renders and plans in your projects are saved in the app’s storage on your iPhone. They are encrypted by iOS while the device is locked and are not included in iCloud backups. The publisher has no access to them.

What is sent to create a render

An image cannot be generated on the iPhone itself. When you start a render, a variant or a retouch, the app sends:

  • the image to transform (the photo you chose, or the render you are retouching), resized;
  • the style brief, a short text describing what you redo, what you keep and the style or colour you picked.

These items are sent over an encrypted connection to the image generation provider Replicate, Inc. (United States), which has them processed by the image model it makes available, only to produce the image you asked for. The resulting image is then downloaded and saved in your project, on your iPhone.

The app attaches no name, email address or advertising identifier to this upload: it has none. As with any internet connection, the provider sees the device’s IP address. It handles the data it receives under its own terms, described in its privacy policy.

This upload involves a transfer of data to the United States. It only happens at your request, for each render, and it is necessary to provide you with the image. Avoid photographing recognisable people or personal documents.

Test version

This upload only happens if a render key has been saved in the Account tab. Without a key, the app shows sample renders prepared in advance and no photo leaves the iPhone. The key is kept in the iPhone keychain and is only sent to the rendering provider.

The upload goes straight from the iPhone to Replicate, without passing through a server run by the publisher. The image model used is a Google model: to produce the image, Replicate may pass it on to Google LLC (United States), which handles it under its own terms.

Permissions requested

  • Camera. Used only when you take a photo or start a 3D scan.
  • Photos. To import an image, the app uses the iOS picker: it only receives the photo you choose and has no access to the rest of your library. To save a render or a video, it only asks for permission to add an item to Photos, at the moment you request it.

You can withdraw these permissions at any time in Settings, then Deco Render.

Purchases

Deco Render Pro subscriptions are purchased, billed and managed by Apple. The publisher receives neither your name, nor your address, nor your payment details. The app only reads, on your device, the subscription status provided by Apple in order to remove the watermark.

Apple gives the publisher aggregated sales statistics. If you agreed, in your iPhone settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may also pass on crash reports and usage statistics that do not identify you.

What Deco Render does not do

  • No account, no sign-up, no profile.
  • No advertising and no selling of data.
  • No trackers, no analytics and no audience measurement built into the app.
  • The publisher does not keep your photos or your projects.

Retention and deletion

Your projects stay on your iPhone for as long as you keep them. You can delete a project from its page, in the Projects tab: its photos and renders are erased. Deleting the app erases all projects. Images you saved to Photos or shared no longer depend on the app and are deleted wherever you put them.

Test version

If you saved a render key, remove it in Account, then Render key, before deleting the app.

Your rights

The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) give you the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to object to its processing or ask for it to be restricted, and to receive it in a common format.

Because the publisher keeps neither your photos nor your projects, most of these rights are exercised directly on your iPhone, by deleting a project or the app. For any other request, use the contact given above. You can also turn to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) in Switzerland, or to the data protection authority of your country of residence in the European Union.

Children

Deco Render is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data about them.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no audience measurement tool, and no fonts or scripts from another service. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States), which may record technical connection data, including visitors’ IP addresses, to keep the service secure.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the way the app works changes, for example when a new feature arrives. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
