Your interior, restyled before you touch a thing.

Tap what you redo. Hold what you keep.

Your photo is split into zones right on the iPhone: sofa, floor, walls, roof, lawn. You point, and the brief writes itself.

Scan the room. Walk away with its floor plan.

With the iPhone’s LiDAR sensor, Deco Render measures the walls, doors, windows and furniture in about a minute. Everything is computed on the device.

  • Dimensioned planThe walls, the openings and their dimensions, seen from above.
  • 3D modelThe room in 3D, which you can spin with a fingertip.
  • QuantitiesFloor area, wall paint, skirting boards, ceiling height.
  • Indicative budgetA range computed from the quantities and from sample prices, to be confirmed by a tradesperson.
  • PDF reportPlan, quantities, budget and before/after comparison, in a single document to send.

3D scanning requires an iPhone with LiDAR (Pro models, from iPhone 12 Pro) and is for interiors. Without LiDAR, a sample room that has already been measured lets you browse the plan and the budget.

A single difference: the watermark.

Deco Render Pro is a yearly or weekly subscription. No free trial that turns into a subscription.

Subscriptions renew automatically and are managed in your Apple account settings, where you can cancel at any time. Prices are shown in the app before any purchase. Details are in the terms of use.

What leaves your iPhone: the photo to transform and its brief.

No account to create, no ads, no trackers.

Stays on your iPhone

Reading the photo
Splitting the photo into zones and recognising the type of room are done by on-device models. Nothing is sent for this step.
The 3D scan
Measurements, plan, quantities and budget are computed on the device.
Your projects
Your photos, renders and plans stay in the app, on your device. Deleting the app erases them.

Leaves your iPhone

For a render
The image to transform and the style brief go to the image generation provider, only to produce the image.
For a purchase
The subscription is paid through Apple. Deco Render never sees any payment data.
