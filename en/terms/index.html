1. Purpose

These terms govern the use of the Deco Render app for iPhone, published by an independent developer based in Switzerland (“the publisher”). By installing or using the app, you accept them. They supplement the licence agreement mentioned in section 10.

2. What the app does

Deco Render proposes images of your interior, facade or garden in another style, from a photo or a 3D scan, while aiming to keep the real structure of the place. The images are produced by an artificial intelligence model. For a scanned room, the app can also produce a dimensioned plan, a 3D model, quantities, an indicative budget and a PDF file.

Creating a render requires an internet connection and relies on a third-party provider. 3D scanning requires an iPhone with a LiDAR sensor. Features marked “Coming soon” are not available yet and come with no commitment on dates.

3. Free plan and Deco Render Pro

The app is free to use. The free plan includes 3 renders on your own photos, unlimited renders of the sample photo, styles, 3D scanning, the floor plan and the PDF file; exported renders and videos carry a watermark. The Deco Render Pro subscription gives you renders on your photos, retouches included, up to 50 per 7-day period on the weekly plan or 60 per 30-day period on the yearly plan, and removes the watermark. These limits protect the service from abuse; the app shows how many renders remain.

  • Durations. Deco Render Pro is offered as a yearly or weekly subscription, with no free trial period. The price of each plan is shown in the app before any purchase.
  • Payment. Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm the purchase.
  • Automatic renewal. The subscription renews automatically for the same duration and at the same price, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Cancellation. You manage and cancel the subscription at any time in Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid for; deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.
  • Refunds. Purchases are processed by Apple. Refund requests are made to Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

4. Generated images

The images produced by Deco Render are proposals meant to inspire. They are not construction drawings, technical documents or a guarantee of feasibility. A render may alter or interpret details of your photo, show materials, furniture or plants that do not exist as such, or fail to comply with applicable building rules.

Before committing to works or purchases, have your project validated by qualified professionals and check which permits are required, in particular for a facade or an outdoor project.

5. Measurements, quantities and budgets
